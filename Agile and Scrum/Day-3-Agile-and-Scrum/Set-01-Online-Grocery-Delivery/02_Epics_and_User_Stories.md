# Task 2 – Epics and User Stories

## Project: Online Grocery Delivery Application

## 1. Introduction

An epic is a large body of work that represents a major functionality of a product. It is divided into smaller, manageable user stories that can be developed and delivered incrementally.

For the Online Grocery Delivery Application, the product backlog is divided into three major epics:

1. Browse and Search Products
2. Shopping Cart and Checkout
3. Order and Delivery Tracking

A total of 12 user stories have been created, with four stories under each epic.

---

## 2. Epic 1 – Browse and Search Products

**Epic Description:**

This epic focuses on helping customers discover grocery products, explore categories, search for specific items, and view product information before making a purchase.

### User Stories

**US-01: Browse Grocery Categories**

As a customer, I want to browse groceries by categories such as fruits, vegetables, dairy, and beverages, so that I can easily find the products I need.

**US-02: Search for Products**

As a customer, I want to search for grocery products using product names, so that I can quickly locate specific items without browsing every category.

**US-03: View Product Details**

As a customer, I want to view product details such as price, description, quantity, and availability, so that I can make informed purchasing decisions.

**US-04: Filter and Sort Products**

As a customer, I want to filter products by price and availability and sort them by price, so that I can find products that match my preferences and budget.

---

## 3. Epic 2 – Shopping Cart and Checkout

**Epic Description:**

This epic focuses on allowing customers to select grocery products, manage their shopping carts, review order details, and complete purchases through a secure checkout process.

### User Stories

**US-05: Add Products to Cart**

As a customer, I want to add grocery products to my shopping cart, so that I can collect all the items I intend to purchase.

**US-06: Update Cart Quantity**

As a customer, I want to increase or decrease the quantity of products in my cart, so that I can adjust my order according to my requirements.

**US-07: Remove Products from Cart**

As a customer, I want to remove unwanted products from my shopping cart, so that I can correct my order before checkout.

**US-08: Make Secure Payments**

As a customer, I want to pay for my grocery order using available payment methods, so that I can complete my purchase securely and conveniently.

---

## 4. Epic 3 – Order and Delivery Tracking

**Epic Description:**

This epic focuses on helping customers place orders successfully, monitor delivery progress, and receive updates about their grocery orders.

### User Stories

**US-09: Place a Grocery Order**

As a customer, I want to confirm my shopping cart and place a grocery order, so that my selected products can be prepared for delivery.

**US-10: Select a Delivery Slot**

As a customer, I want to choose an available delivery date and time slot, so that I can receive my groceries at a convenient time.

**US-11: Track Order Status**

As a customer, I want to track my order status, so that I can know whether my order is confirmed, being prepared, dispatched, or delivered.

**US-12: Receive Order Notifications**

As a customer, I want to receive notifications about order confirmation and delivery updates, so that I stay informed about my grocery order.

## 6. Conclusion

The product backlog contains three major epics divided into 12 user stories.

Each story is written from the customer's perspective and describes a specific functionality along with its expected benefit.

These stories provide a foundation for the next stages of backlog refinement, INVEST evaluation, acceptance criteria, prioritisation, and story-point estimation.
