# Task 1 – Product Vision and Waterfall vs Agile Comparison

## Project: Insurance Claims Portal

## 1. Product Vision Statement

To develop a secure, reliable, and user-friendly self-service insurance claims portal that enables policyholders to register claims, upload supporting documents, monitor claim progress, and receive settlement notifications online.

The portal aims to simplify the insurance claims process, reduce manual paperwork, improve transparency, and provide faster communication between customers and insurance providers.

### Target Users

- Insurance policyholders
- Insurance claims officers
- Surveyors and assessors
- Insurance administrators

### Business Goals

- Simplify the claim registration process.
- Reduce manual documentation and processing.
- Improve claim status transparency.
- Enable faster communication with customers.
- Improve customer satisfaction and operational efficiency.

## 2. Waterfall vs Agile Comparison

| Aspect | Waterfall | Agile |
|---|---|---|
| Approach | Sequential development | Iterative and incremental development |
| Requirements | Defined mainly at the beginning | Can evolve through feedback |
| Customer involvement | Mainly during initial and final stages | Continuous involvement |
| Testing | Often performed after development | Performed throughout development |
| Changes | Difficult and costly to accommodate | Changes can be incorporated through backlog refinement |
| Delivery | Usually delivered at the end | Delivered incrementally |

## 3. Why Agile Suits the Insurance Claims Portal

Agile is suitable for this programme because insurance requirements, customer expectations, and regulatory needs may change during development.

An iterative approach allows the team to deliver important features such as claim registration, document uploads, claim tracking, and settlement notifications incrementally.

Continuous stakeholder feedback helps identify usability issues and improve the portal throughout development. Regular testing also helps identify defects early and maintain reliability and security.

Therefore, Agile provides the flexibility, continuous feedback, and incremental delivery needed to develop an effective insurance claims portal.

## 4. Conclusion

The Insurance Claims Portal will improve the customer experience by simplifying claim registration, document submission, and claim tracking.

Using Agile allows the Scrum Team to develop the portal in manageable increments, respond to changing requirements, and continuously improve product quality.
