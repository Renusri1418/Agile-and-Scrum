# Task 1 – Product Vision and Stakeholder Mapping

## 1. Product Vision Statement

To develop a user-friendly and reliable mobile application that enables patients to conveniently book, reschedule, and cancel doctor appointments, receive timely reminders, and access available doctor schedules. The application aims to reduce manual appointment management, minimise scheduling conflicts, improve patient satisfaction, and help hospitals manage appointments efficiently.

## 2. Project Stakeholders

The Hospital Appointment Booking Application involves the following stakeholders:

| Stakeholder | Responsibility / Interest |
|---|---|
| Patients | Book, reschedule, and cancel appointments and receive reminders. |
| Doctors | Manage availability and view scheduled appointments. |
| Hospital Management | Define business goals and monitor application performance. |
| Receptionists | Assist patients and coordinate appointment schedules. |
| Development Team | Design, develop, test, and maintain the application. |
| Scrum Master | Facilitate Scrum activities and remove team impediments. |
| Product Owner | Prioritise requirements and maximise product value. |

## 3. Mapping Stakeholders to Scrum Roles

| Stakeholder | Scrum Role | Reason |
|---|---|---|
| Hospital Management Representative | Product Owner | Represents business needs and defines product priorities. |
| Product Manager / Hospital Representative | Product Owner | Maintains the product backlog and communicates the product vision. |
| Scrum Facilitator | Scrum Master | Ensures Scrum practices are followed and helps remove impediments. |
| Mobile Application Developers | Developers | Build and implement the mobile application. |
| Backend Developers | Developers | Develop APIs, database operations, and appointment services. |
| UI/UX Designers | Developers | Design the user interface and improve usability. |
| QA Engineers | Developers | Test application functionality and verify quality. |
| Patients | Customers | Use the application to manage their appointments. |
| Doctors | Customers | Use the system to manage availability and view appointments. |
| Receptionists | Customers | Use the system to coordinate and manage patient appointments. |

## 4. Responsibilities of the Scrum Roles

### Product Owner
- Defines and communicates the product vision.
- Prioritises the product backlog.
- Ensures that requirements provide business and customer value.
- Clarifies requirements for the development team.

### Scrum Master
- Facilitates Scrum events.
- Helps the team follow Scrum principles.
- Identifies and removes impediments.
- Promotes collaboration and continuous improvement.

### Developers
- Design and develop application features.
- Estimate and implement user stories.
- Perform testing and quality checks.
- Deliver a usable product increment during each sprint.

## 5. Expected Project Benefits

- Convenient online appointment booking.
- Reduced waiting time and manual scheduling.
- Fewer appointment conflicts.
- Easy rescheduling and cancellation.
- Timely appointment reminders.
- Improved coordination between patients, doctors, and hospital staff.

## Conclusion

The Hospital Appointment Booking Application aims to improve the appointment management experience for patients and hospitals. By applying Scrum, the team can develop the application incrementally, receive regular feedback, and adapt to changing requirements.
