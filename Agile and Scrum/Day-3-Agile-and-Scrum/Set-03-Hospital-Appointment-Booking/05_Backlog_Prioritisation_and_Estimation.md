## 4. Planning Poker and Story Point Estimation

Planning Poker is an estimation technique in which team members independently estimate the effort and complexity of user stories. They discuss differences in estimates and agree on a final story point value.

## 5. Story Points vs Hours

| Story Points | Hours |
|---|---|
| Measure relative effort, complexity and uncertainty. | Measure the actual time spent on a task. |
| Used for estimating and comparing user stories. | Used for tracking time and scheduling work. |
| Do not represent a fixed number of hours. | Represent a specific duration. |
| Help the team understand the relative size of work. | Help the team understand how long work takes. |

### Example

A user story estimated at 5 story points is considered more complex than a story estimated at 2 points. However, 5 story points do not mean 5 hours of work.

## 6. Conclusion

The product backlog has been prioritised based on business value and effort. Planning Poker has been used to provide sample story point estimates. Story points help the Scrum team estimate relative complexity, while hours are used to measure actual time.
