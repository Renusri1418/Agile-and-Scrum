# Task 3 – INVEST Evaluation of User Stories

## Project: Online Grocery Delivery Application

## 1. Introduction

The INVEST checklist is used to evaluate the quality of user stories and ensure that they are suitable for development within an Agile Sprint.

INVEST stands for:

- **I – Independent:** The story should be as independent as possible from other stories.
- **N – Negotiable:** The story should allow discussion between the Product Owner and Developers.
- **V – Valuable:** The story should provide clear value to the customer or business.
- **E – Estimable:** The team should be able to estimate the effort required.
- **S – Small:** The story should be small enough to complete within a Sprint.
- **T – Testable:** The story should have clear conditions that can be verified.

- The phrase "available payment methods" does not specify the payment scope.
- Different payment methods may involve significantly different implementation efforts.
- The story does not define a clear, testable payment outcome.
