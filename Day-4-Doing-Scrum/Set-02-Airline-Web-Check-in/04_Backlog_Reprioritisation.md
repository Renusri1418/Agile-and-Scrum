# Task 4 – Backlog Reprioritisation After the New Request

## 1. Scenario

On Day 4 of the 10-day sprint, the business requests a new feature: **Refund to Wallet**.

This feature allows users to receive refunds directly into their digital wallet instead of waiting for another payment method.

## 2. Impact of the New Request

The new request has business value because it improves the refund experience and makes funds available to customers through their wallet.

However, adding new work during a sprint can affect the team's capacity and its ability to achieve the Sprint Goal.

## 3. Backlog Changes

| Action | User Story | Story Points | Reason |
|---|---|---:|---|
| Add | US-08 – Request a refund to wallet | 5 | New business requirement |
| Remove from current sprint | US-07 – Download transaction statement | 5 | Lower immediate priority |
| Retain | US-01 – Register and log in | 5 | Essential wallet functionality |
| Retain | US-02 – View wallet balance | 3 | Core wallet functionality |
| Retain | US-03 – Add money | 5 | Core wallet functionality |
| Retain | US-04 – View transaction history | 3 | Supports transaction visibility |
| Retain | US-06 – Receive transaction notifications | 2 | Important user communication |

## 4. Updated Sprint Backlog

| User Story | Story Points |
|---|---:|
| US-01 – Register and log in | 5 |
| US-02 – View wallet balance | 3 |
| US-03 – Add money | 5 |
| US-04 – View transaction history | 3 |
| US-06 – Receive transaction notifications | 2 |
| US-08 – Request a refund to wallet | 5 |
| **Total** | **23** |

The new feature replaces a lower-priority story, keeping the sprint scope at 23 points.

## 5. Who Decides?

- **Product Owner:** Responsible for ordering the Product Backlog and discussing business priorities.
- **Developers:** Decide how much work can realistically be completed and how to incorporate changes without compromising quality.
- **Scrum Master:** Helps facilitate the discussion and ensures Scrum practices are followed.

The Product Owner and Developers collaborate on any changes to the Sprint Backlog. The Sprint Goal should not be compromised by simply adding more work.

## 6. How the Sprint Goal Is Protected

- Keep the original Sprint Goal focused on essential wallet operations.
- Replace lower-priority work instead of simply increasing the workload.
- Discuss the new requirement with the Developers.
- Maintain the Definition of Done for every story.
- If the Sprint Goal becomes obsolete, the Product Owner may cancel the sprint.

## 7. Conclusion

The refund-to-wallet feature is added by replacing the lower-priority transaction statement feature. The team keeps the sprint workload at 23 points and protects its Sprint Goal while responding to the new business requirement.
